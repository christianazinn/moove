# ios/ - SwiftUI app + MooveKit

Lanes: ios-capture (capture, review, sync, auth), ios-newhome (new home, fly-in, summary, logistics), commerce (spending log, approvals, shopping screens).

- **XcodeGen**: the project is defined in `ios/project.yml`, and `*.xcodeproj` is gitignored. Run `xcodegen` after pulling or adding files. Never commit the `.xcodeproj`.
- Target iOS 17+, Swift 6, SwiftUI.
- Feature folders live under `Moove/Features/<Feature>/` (Auth, Scan, Room, Inventory, Review, Agents, Spending, Approvals, Listings, Shopping, Move, NewHome, Pack). Stay in your own folders to avoid conflicts. Shared UI goes in `Moove/Shared/`.
- **`MooveKit/`** is a local Swift package for models mirroring `docs/data-model.md` plus pure logic (layout solver, volume/truck estimate, packing). No UIKit, RealityKit or RoomPlan imports, so it builds and tests on Linux (`swift test`). Put anything testable here.
- **Object detector model** (#82): `MooveObjects.mlpackage` (Ultralytics YOLOE-11L with 127 household prompts baked in, AGPL-3.0) isn't in git. `scripts/fetch-models.sh` (xcodegen's `preGenCommand`) downloads it sha256-pinned from this repo's `models-v1` prerelease into the gitignored `Moove/Resources/Models/`. Without it the extra detector is off; RoomPlan and auto photos still work. Its labels map to Moove categories in MooveKit `ObjectVocabulary`, so keep the two in sync when re-exporting. Through Vision, a class score is `label.confidence * observation.confidence`, because Vision rescales the scores to sum to 1. Detector boxes (#267): `SightingMerger` refits each object from the points of all its sightings and confirms it only after 2 distinct views (≥0.3 s or ≥15° apart), unless one view scored ≥0.85 (≥0.5 m), or ≥0.7 for a ≥1 m thing RoomPlan never finds. A find whose footprint overlaps a RoomPlan object's by ≥25% of the smaller footprint (and shares height) is a duplicate. `DetectorPlacement` pads the depth to `ObjectVocabulary.minimumDepth` away from the mean camera position (LiDAR saw the front) and turns the front (+z) away from a wall within 0.3 m, else toward the room's center.
- RoomPlan needs a physical LiDAR iPhone. Keep a fixture scan in `agents/fixtures/` for Simulator and preview work.
- SpacetimeDB from Swift: community SDK `ekscrypto/spacetimedb-swift-sdk` (spike passed, no gateway; see `docs/decisions/0020-swift-spacetimedb-sdk.md`). Screens only use `MooveStore` (`Moove/Sync/`), which drives a `SyncBackend`: `SpacetimeBackend` (live) or MooveKit's `FixtureBackend` (offline demo, previews, tests). How to regenerate the Swift bindings: `Moove/Sync/README.md`.

- **Crop classifier recheck:** after the auto photos, `ScanSession` runs `Features/Scan/ObjectClassifier.swift` (Meta PE-Core-S/16-384 image tower, Apache-2.0, 8-bit Core ML `ObjectClassifier.mlpackage`, ~23 MB, gitignored like the detector; `fetch-models.sh` gets it from the `models-v2` prerelease when that exists, else the recheck is off) on each **detector** object's auto-photo framing plus up to 3 tight keyframe crops (`AutoPhotographer.classifierCrops`, `CropPicker.top`), averages the class probabilities, and MooveKit `ObjectRecheck.check` (head + label matrix in MooveKit `Resources/ObjectClassifierLabels.*`, `ObjectClassifierHead`) keeps, relabels, rejects (wall/door/clutter or a fixture: radiator, shower curtain, towel rack) or marks it `other`. RoomPlan-origin objects (by origin, not category) only get the reject-only `ObjectRecheck.checkRoomPlan` (none + fixtures ≥ 0.7; never relabeled; sink/toilet/bathtub/stove/oven/dishwasher/fireplace/stairs skipped). Size priors (`ObjectSanity.footprints`) only veto clear mismatches (>2× outside). The head also has image-prototype rows (category `clutter`: mop, broom, dustpan, squeegee, plunger, tripod/light stand; `cart`: hand truck); `check` sets clutter aside (renormalizing the rest), rejects at clutter ≥ 0.8 with vacuum < 0.1 and the detector's category < 0.3, and makes clutter-on-top (≥ 0.5) `other`; a relabel also needs its target at ≥ 0.4 and to fit `ObjectSanity.fitsSizePrior`, and a detector "vacuum" the classifier gives < 0.5 becomes `other`. Detector finds also carry the detector's per-object label history (`SightingMerger.LabelHistory`: every label across frames, incl. the tracks `results()` merged away, stored as `RoomGeometry.Object.measured?.labelHistory`, optional so old scans decode); `ObjectRecheck.check(..., history:)` pools it with the classifier (β = 1, non-item mass untouched) and rejects when the detector flipped labels (top label < 67% of ≥ 2 views) and none + fixtures ≥ 0.4. Thresholds come from an optional fitted `policy` block in `ObjectClassifierLabels.json` (`ObjectRecheck.Policy`: temperature, per-category logit bias, reject/doubt bars and mass mode per origin, per-detector relabel margins; absent = `Policy.shipped`, today's constants); a non-item mass in its doubt band gives `.doubt` (kept, tagged `Recheck.suspect`, which goes to `import_scan_v2` and lets vision veto at medium confidence; naming and voxel models ignore `Recheck.*`). Rejects are listed in the finished screen's skipped notice (`ScanSkips.combined`); `submit` waits for the recheck. A user's "Name it" wins. ## ios-newhome lane
- **Item 3D is voxel only** ([decision 0108](../docs/decisions/0108-voxel-item-models.md)). Every 3D view draws items as procedural 5 cm voxel models. Don't add USDZ/GLB item models. Shape comes from the user's fix-it kind (`UserKind.*`) first, then RoomPlan attributes, then name/material/brand words, then the scanned size, and the size vetoes a name that can't be right (`VoxelModels+Sanity.swift`: per-kind envelopes, re-route by shape); `condition` sets wear; measured colors (`Item.colors`, or `ScanColors` in-session) override the keyword palette. Seeded randomness only picks small details.
- **Where the voxel pipeline lives:**
  - MooveKit `Sources/MooveKit/Voxel/`: `VoxelGrid.swift` (`VoxelItemSpec`, built from an `Item`), `VoxelModels*.swift` (routing + builders), `VoxelHouse.swift` (the home shell: base, floors, cut-away walls, doors, windows; `buildLayers` splits off the exterior walls above the 1.1 m cut per facing), `VoxelMesher.swift` (greedy meshing into one mesh per piece).
  - MooveKit `NewHome/PropPlacer.swift`: puts small items on free furniture surfaces (`VoxelModels.surfaces`) and hangs art/mirrors/wall TVs on walls; anything you sit on (ottoman, bench, chair, sofa, bed: `floorKinds`) stays on the floor at any size and holds nothing (`seatKinds`), so a scanned ottoman tucked under a table isn't lifted onto it; `Placement` carries optional `elevationM` / `onTopOf` / `wallMounted`. Pass `scanned:` (ids whose placement came from the scan) so those keep their scanned x/y: a scanned prop only goes onto a surface whose footprint holds its spot (±15 cm), a scanned wall item stays at its spot on its nearest wall. `RoomGeometryLoader` prefers this device's `Documents/scans/*/geometry.json` (matched by item `scanObjectId`s) and moves stale tunnel `/files/` URLs onto the current upload base.
  - MooveKit `Room/ColorSampler.swift`: wall, floor and per-object colors from the scan's camera keyframes (`ScanColors`). The frames are kept by `Moove/Features/Scan/ScanFrameRecorder.swift`.
  - MooveKit `NewHome/` designer layout ([decision 0300](../docs/decisions/0300-new-home-layout-design.md)): `LayoutSolver` keeps its API but delegates to `Designer` (+`Designer+Rooms.swift`): `DesignRole` classifies pieces (voxel kind + `PropPlacer.role` + name words; RoomPlan's plain "storage" box by shape; toilet/sink/bathtub by category words), beds go one per bedroom (twin/bunk pairs may share, extras in `LayoutOption.stored`, never the living room; a one-room plan takes all), then function groups are placed anchor first with a small deterministic cost search (bed headboard on a solid wall with nightstands either side and the dresser facing it; sofa facing the TV at 1.5–2.5× the diagonal with the coffee table 0.35–0.45 m in front, armchairs at its ends, lamp at the sofa's end, rug under it, TV set on its stand; dining table centered with chairs around it and 0.9 m clear; desk by a window with its chair), and `WalkGrid` (door flood fill, 10 cm cells) keeps doors connected and each piece's use side reachable. Small things are placed next to their parent so `PropPlacer` lifts them onto it. Common sense rules: only bathroom things go in a bathroom (`Designer.belongsInBathroom`: fixtures, towel racks/hampers, plants, bins, mirrors, art); anything else that fits no other room goes to storage. A wall TV hangs on the wall the sofa (or the foot of the bed) faces, and an ottoman/bench stays with the seats or the bed. Styles differ by room use and clearances (`LayoutSolver.rules`). Look at layouts with `LayoutPreview.svg`: `MOOVE_LAYOUT_SVG_DIR=/out swift test --filter LayoutPreviewTests` (Docker: add `-v <dir>:/out -e MOOVE_LAYOUT_SVG_DIR=/out`), then `qlmanage -t -s 900 -o . *.svg`. `MovingRooms` decides which old rooms move (default newest scan per room name; Move's one **Moving from** row (`Features/NewHome/MovingRoomsPicker.swift`: `MovingFromRow`, `MovingRoomsSheet`; identifiers `pack.movingFrom` / `pack.chooseRooms`) opens a checklist with Select all / None, saved in UserDefaults `moove.newHome.rooms`). One source of truth: `MooveStore.movingRoomPicks` / `movingChoices` / `movingItems` (Pack: `movingItems(where:)`, which adds undecided sentimental items), so the layout, boxes, furniture notes and truck agree; bought items stay "Delivered". `MattressPairing`: a mattress whose footprint is within 15% of a bed (25% for one named a frame; not bunks) is laid on it (same spot, `onTopOf` the bed, raised) instead of counting as a second bed; `FurnitureLayer` skips drawing it (`drawnWithBed`), since the bed model has a mattress. New Home solves off the main actor (`LayoutSolver.arrange` → `arrangeAll`: every style + PropPlacer) with "Arranging your home…" over the stage, cancelled when the plan or items change, and caches results per `SolveKey` (plan, items) in `LayoutCache` so re-opening is instant.
  - MooveKit `NewHome/`: `FloorPlan`, `LayoutSolver` (three styles), `DemoHouse`, `ManualFloorPlan`, `ListingImport.swift` (`ListingLink.parse`, no network; `EstimatedFloorPlan` from beds/baths/sqft). Packing and the truck estimate are in `Packing/`.
  - App `Features/NewHome/`: `VoxelEntity.swift` (mesh -> RealityKit entity, cached per spec), `FurnitureLayer.swift` (placed items + fly-in), `NewHomeScene.swift` (the house shell + room labels), `FloorPlan3DView.swift` (takes sampled `wallColor`/`floorColor`), `VoxelThumbnail.swift`, `ListingImportView.swift` + `ListingImportClient.swift` (worker `POST /homes/import`). The scan's dollhouse is `Moove/Shared/ScanResultsView.swift`, fed by `Features/Scan/ScanFinishedResults.swift` and `Features/Room/RoomGeometry+Dollhouse.swift`.
- **Dollhouse walls:** interior walls are cut at 1.1 m (`NewHomeScene.cutawayHeightM`). Each exterior facing has two looks, split off the base by `VoxelHouse.buildLayers` into `Layer` pieces (`tall` or low, with the `facings` they belong to): tall is one continuous wall up to `VoxelHouse.displayWallHeightM` (the plan's `wallHeightM`, a scan's measured height, 2.6 m for the demo house; a scan over 3.0 m is drawn at 2.8 m) with windows glazed from the sill to a single frame at 2.05 m (`openingTopM`), doors open to 2.05 m and curtains/rods hung at the window head; low is today's cut-away look (cap row at 1.1 m, window frame bar under it, curtains from a rod at the cut). The base holds only voxels both looks share, so neither look shows the other's cap/frame rows, and `base` + low pieces == `VoxelHouse.build`. Corner columns shared by two facings are their own piece (both facings), never duplicated. `FloorPlan3DView`'s coordinator calls `NewHomeScene.setViewDirection` on every camera update (drag, auto-orbit, reset, reframe), which only toggles `isEnabled` by `Layer.isShown`: a tall piece is up while any of its facings points away from the camera (`Facing.isBack`, dot < -0.2), its low twin otherwise, so the back walls stand full height and the near ones stay low. `VoxelHouseLayersTests` checks no voxel is drawn twice at any yaw. Interior partitions always stay at the cut (a tall one would hide the room behind it). Wall art (`PropPlacer.wallTopM`) stays under the cut on every wall, since any exterior wall can become a near wall.

- **Render budget (#224):** MooveKit `RenderBudgetTests` meshes the demo items, every voxel kind at its default size, the demo bedroom dollhouse and the demo house with its laid-out items. Today (triangles; the shell counts the base plus the wall pieces shown at the worst camera yaw): biggest item the bed ~10.7k, bedroom 27.6k (shell 3.5k + 12 items), house 38.4k (shell 15.7k + 8 items), all built in ~0.4-1.1 s on Linux debug. Budgets: 20k per item, 60k bedroom, 50k house, 5 s build. If a change trips one, shrink the geometry (fewer color speckles, merged faces) before raising the number; `report()` prints the current numbers.
- **Pack boxes:** MooveKit `PackingPlanner.consolidate` fills boxes after the first per-room/kind/fragility grouping (compatible kinds share, soft things pad fragile ones, small → medium → large before adding a box, a box under 40% full joins another, then any two boxes of a room the rules allow together share one box that holds both, so big boxes fill first; never books with fragile, kitchen/bathroom only with their own kind, the tote and keep-with-you box alone). Typical everyday lines are `BoxContent.estimated` (`MovingBox.estimatedOnly`) and sized for a light one-bedroom/student move (`PackingTemplates`, Lisa: fewer boxes; the living room has no typical books, so they land in the bedroom or office). Typical lines count once per room **kind** (`PackingPlanner.typicalUnits`): the first Bedroom gets the full set, "Bedroom 2"/"Bedroom 3" (or a kind a sibling's scan already found) about a third (`extraRoomShare`), and typical books go to at most one room per kind, once per scanned room. Scanned things that are already a container or ride loose (`asIsCategories`: box, bin, basket, suitcase, backpack, bag, trash can, fan, vacuum, rug, bike, ...) are furniture notes ("Rides as is"), never boxed; laptops and monitors pack as electronics, mirrors as decor. Lisa's three bedroom scans gave 47 boxes; `PackingSameKindRoomsTests` (24 before) is now 9. The demo is 6 boxes (bedroom 2: the scanned lamp shade is fragile, so books and clothes ride apart; living room, kitchen dish pack, tote, keep-with-you). **Box cap** (`PackingPlanner.boxCap` = 12, Lisa): over it, `consolidate` also merges any two boxes of one room the safety rules allow (towels may join the bathroom box), then lets boxes of rooms of one kind (Bedroom + Bedroom 2) share at any fill, then puts leftovers under 60% full (`miscFill`) from different rooms into a **Misc** box (`miscRoom`; each line keeps its room in `DraftLine.room`, so its reason still says where it goes). Weight and fragile limits never bend, so a whole two-bedroom home stays over 12 (`PackingBoxCapTests` explains the floor). Move's `BoxesSection` shows a heading per room (`pack.room.<room>`, `pack.room.openFirst`) over a grid of its cards (`pack.box.<id>`), scanned boxes first, estimate-only ones after.
- **Truck in 3D** (on Move's `TruckCard` under Getting there, non-interactive `TruckSceneView(interactive: false)`; tapping it opens `Features/Pack/TruckView.swift`): MooveKit `Packing/TruckLoader.swift` loads the plan's on-truck furniture (`plan.furniture` minus "Keep with you" car pieces) and boxes (minus keep-with-you) into `TruckSize.cargoM` (inner L×W×H per size): greedy extreme-point packing, furniture biggest first against the cab, mattresses and bed frames on edge, other furniture tips only when no upright spot fits (appliances never), boxes heaviest first then fragile then the open-first tote by the door; only boxes stack, on boxes or flat-topped case goods, never on a lighter fragile box. Without an explicit truck it moves up a size when shapes don't fit the volume estimate (the demo plan is a cargo van by volume, a 10 ft truck in 3D; `TruckLoad.sizeNote(planned:)` then shows "Fits a cargo van by volume; packs best in a 10 ft truck." on both the truck card and the 3D legend, and `TruckCard` hands its load to TruckView so both show the same truck). `Voxel/VoxelTruck.swift` builds the cut-away voxel truck (`VoxelTruck.frame/center(of:in:)` map cargo coords to the grid; cargo x = 0 is the cab wall) and `VoxelMovingBox` the boxes, taped in `PackingPalette.color(forRoom:)` (the same room colors as the 2D box cards and labels). `TruckRenderBudgetTests`: shells ≤ 4k triangles (26 ft ~1.1k), demo load ~19.4k ≤ 60k.
- **MooveKit tests without Swift 6.2 locally:** run them in Docker. Docker Desktop can't mount `/private/tmp`, so mount the repo from your home folder, and copy the sources out of the read-only mount without `.build`:
  ```sh
  docker run --rm -v <repo>/ios/MooveKit:/src:ro swift:6.2 bash -c 'mkdir /work && cd /src && tar --exclude=.build -cf - . | (cd /work && tar xf -) && cd /work && swift test'
  ```
