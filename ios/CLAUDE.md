# ios/ - SwiftUI app + MooveKit

Lanes: ios-capture (capture, review, sync, auth), ios-newhome (new home, fly-in, summary, logistics), commerce (spending log, approvals, shopping screens).

- **XcodeGen**: the project is defined in `ios/project.yml`, and `*.xcodeproj` is gitignored. Run `xcodegen` after pulling or adding files. Never commit the `.xcodeproj`.
- Target iOS 17+, Swift 6, SwiftUI.
- Feature folders live under `Moove/Features/<Feature>/` (Auth, Scan, Room, Inventory, Review, Agents, Spending, Approvals, Listings, Shopping, NewHome, Pack, Summary). Stay in your own folders to avoid conflicts. Shared UI goes in `Moove/Shared/`.
- **`MooveKit/`** is a local Swift package for models mirroring `docs/data-model.md` plus pure logic (layout solver, volume/truck estimate, packing). No UIKit, RealityKit or RoomPlan imports, so it builds and tests on Linux (`swift test`). Put anything testable here.
- **Object detector model** (#82): `MooveObjects.mlpackage` (Ultralytics YOLOE-11L with 127 household prompts baked in, AGPL-3.0) isn't in git. `scripts/fetch-models.sh` (xcodegen's `preGenCommand`) downloads it sha256-pinned from this repo's `models-v1` prerelease into the gitignored `Moove/Resources/Models/`. Without it the extra detector is off; RoomPlan and auto photos still work. Its labels map to Moove categories in MooveKit `ObjectVocabulary`, so keep the two in sync when re-exporting. Through Vision, a class score is `label.confidence * observation.confidence`, because Vision rescales the scores to sum to 1. Detector boxes (#267): `SightingMerger` refits each object from the points of all its sightings and confirms it only after 2 distinct views (≥0.3 s or ≥15° apart), unless one view scored ≥0.85 (≥0.5 m), or ≥0.7 for a ≥1 m thing RoomPlan never finds. A find whose footprint overlaps a RoomPlan object's by ≥25% of the smaller footprint (and shares height) is a duplicate. `DetectorPlacement` pads the depth to `ObjectVocabulary.minimumDepth` away from the mean camera position (LiDAR saw the front) and turns the front (+z) away from a wall within 0.3 m, else toward the room's center.
- RoomPlan needs a physical LiDAR iPhone. Keep a fixture scan in `agents/fixtures/` for Simulator and preview work.
- SpacetimeDB from Swift: community SDK `ekscrypto/spacetimedb-swift-sdk` (spike passed, no gateway; see `docs/decisions/0020-swift-spacetimedb-sdk.md`). Screens only use `MooveStore` (`Moove/Sync/`), which drives a `SyncBackend`: `SpacetimeBackend` (live) or MooveKit's `FixtureBackend` (offline demo, previews, tests). How to regenerate the Swift bindings: `Moove/Sync/README.md`.
- **Inventory (Items > Inventory, `Features/Inventory/`)** is a small museum: items hang in halls by function, not by room (MooveKit `FunctionHall.of`: name words first, so an office/desk/swivel chair is Work & study, a dining chair Kitchen & dining, a nightstand Sleep; then `VoxelModels.kind`; `FunctionHall.halls` orders by count, "Cabinet of Curiosities" last). Each item is an `ExhibitCard` (`InventoryExhibit.swift`: voxel model on a plinth under a spotlight, a ribbon when `maybeSentimental`, the hall's most valuable piece taller in the masonry; 2 columns on iPhone, 3 on iPad, 1 at AX sizes) with a placard from MooveKit `MuseumLabel` (catalog line, honest "Catalogued <scan date>" since there are no purchase dates, `autoStory` mirroring the worker's sort math). User stories ("Grandma's, 1974") live only on the device (`ItemStories`, UserDefaults `moove.itemStories`; attributes must be `Type.value`, so not there; a synced column would be a contract change), and a year in one becomes "Acquired 1974". Room actions (Share, 3D, ⋯ Add item here / Rename room / Hide room) are on the **Rooms** chips at the top. Identifiers: `inventory.item.<id>` (exhibit; tap opens Item detail, long-press has `ItemFixMenu` and `inventory.delete.<id>`), `inventory.story.<id>`, `inventory.story.field` / `.save`, `inventory.hall.<hall>`, `inventory.addItem`, `inventory.confirmDelete`; the chips keep "Share <room>" and "View room in 3D".
- **Listings (Sell & Buy > Listings, `Features/Listings/`)** is a wall of posters (`ListingPoster.swift`): a `FlyerTotals` header (Live / Listed / Sold), then per status group a masonry wall (MooveKit `PosterWall.deal`; 2 columns on iPhone, 3 on iPad, 1 and no tilt at AX sizes). MooveKit `PosterWall` picks each poster's swatch (by listing id), treatment, banner, pin/tape and ±1.5° tilt deterministically, and `PosterWall.stamp` maps status to a stamp (a simulated post is stamped POSTED, never LIVE). **All poster colors are in MooveKit `Models/PosterPalette.swift`** (`PosterPalette.current`): swap it to restyle the wall. The poster keeps `listing.<id>` and `listing.accessibilitySummary` as its one VoiceOver element; long-press has copy / cross-post / view page / Approve and post; on iOS 18 a poster zooms into `ListingDetailView` (`zoomSource` / `zoomDestination`), which has `listing.approve.<id>` / `listing.deny.<id>`.
- Sharing (#10): owners tap **Share room** in Inventory to show the room's `joinCode`; a second phone uses **Join a room** (`MooveStore.joinRoom(code:)` → `join_room`) and the room streams in via the `room_member` subscription. Screens live in `Moove/Features/Sharing/`. Offline demo code: `DemoFixtures.roommateJoinCode`.
- Per-developer config (signing team, bundle id, local server IP, Auth0 ids) goes in `Config/Local.xcconfig` (gitignored; copy `Local.xcconfig.example`).
- The app has five tabs (Scan · Items · Sell & Buy · Move · Summary, [#129](https://github.com/christianazinn/moove/issues/129)). To add a screen, add its key to a group in `Moove/App/TabGroups.swift` (`screens` + the `content` switch) and keep the screen in your feature folder. Jump to a screen from anywhere with `@AppStorage("moove.tab") var tab` and `tab = "<screen key>"`. Sell & Buy is **Listings · Shopping · Agents** (decision 0129 addendum): `Features/Agents/AgentsView.swift` stacks `ApprovalsSection` (Needs your OK, Shared rooms, Decided: last 3 + Show all) and `SpendingSection` (totals tape, then `ReceiptCarousel`: one receipt per page, newest first, `.viewAligned` paging with the next one peeking, ‹ "2 / 3" › pager `receipts.previous` / `receipts.pager` (VoiceOver adjustable) / `receipts.next`, receipts over 8 lines fold behind "Show all N lines"; tests page with Next before scrolling to a line); the old keys "approvals" / "spending" map to "agents" (`TabGroup.segment(for:)`), and "spending" scrolls to the receipts. The tab badge and the "Agents · N" pill count pending approvals.

Identifiers: `review.deck`, `review.card.<id>`, `review.deck.<keep|sell|donate|trash>`, `review.undo`, `review.allDecided`, `review.startOver`, `review.pile.<d>`, `review.list.<d>`, `review.item.<id>`, `review.change.<id>`.
- **Fix-it (correct a misread scan item):** Item detail's **Fix it** section (`Features/Review/ItemFixSection.swift`) and Inventory rows' long-press menu (`ItemFixMenu`): Rename, **What is it?** chips, **Turn around** / **90°**, **Not a real item** (inline confirm). The choices live in the item's attributes (MooveKit `ItemFix`, which always writes the full list and keeps RoomPlan's): `UserKind.<voxel kind>` wins over everything in `VoxelModels.route` (a rename that names a kind, like "Coffee table", sets it too, so it beats RoomPlan's `StorageType.cabinet`), and `UserRotate.90|180|270` is added to the scanned facing in `RoomGeometry+Dollhouse` placements (Scan page, room Voxel view, fly-in starts). - **Room names (Lisa: "let the user name the room"):** no more blanket "Bedroom". The scan's photo step has a **Room** field (`Features/Scan/ScanRoomNameField.swift`, `scan.roomName`, chips `scan.roomName.chip.<name>` incl. `Other`, `scan.roomName.useNumbered`) prefilled by MooveKit `RoomNames.guess` (beds → Bedroom; stove/oven/fridge/dishwasher or sink + counter → Kitchen; toilet/tub/shower, or a sink alone → Bathroom; washer/dryer → Laundry; sofa/TV → Living room; desk/swivel chair + screen → Office; table + ≥3 chairs or dining chairs → Dining room; bike/tools/mower → Garage; else "Room") and `RoomNames.numbered` ("Bedroom 2" when a visible room has the name; the user may keep it, which is how a re-scan replaces a room in `MovingRooms`). It goes into `ScanSession.roomName` → `import_scan_v2`. The overrides are applied to `snapshot` itself (`SyncSnapshot.renamingRooms`), so every screen showing `room.name` shows them, and dropped once the synced name matches. `store.displayName(of:)` reads it.

## ios-newhome lane
- **Item 3D is voxel only** ([decision 0108](../docs/decisions/0108-voxel-item-models.md)). Every 3D view draws items as procedural 5 cm voxel models. Don't add USDZ/GLB item models. Shape comes from the user's fix-it kind (`UserKind.*`) first, then RoomPlan attributes, then name/material/brand words, then the scanned size, and the size vetoes a name that can't be right (`VoxelModels+Sanity.swift`: per-kind envelopes, re-route by shape); `condition` sets wear; measured colors (`Item.colors`, or `ScanColors` in-session) override the keyword palette. Seeded randomness only picks small details.
- **Where the voxel pipeline lives:**
  - MooveKit `Sources/MooveKit/Voxel/`: `VoxelGrid.swift` (`VoxelItemSpec`, built from an `Item`), `VoxelModels*.swift` (routing + builders), `VoxelHouse.swift` (the home shell: base, floors, cut-away walls, doors, windows; `buildLayers` splits off the exterior walls above the 1.1 m cut per facing), `VoxelMesher.swift` (greedy meshing into one mesh per piece).
  - MooveKit `NewHome/PropPlacer.swift`: puts small items on free furniture surfaces (`VoxelModels.surfaces`) and hangs art/mirrors/wall TVs on walls; `Placement` carries optional `elevationM` / `onTopOf` / `wallMounted`. Pass `scanned:` (ids whose placement came from the scan) so those keep their scanned x/y: a scanned prop only goes onto a surface whose footprint holds its spot (±15 cm), a scanned wall item stays at its spot on its nearest wall. `RoomGeometryLoader` prefers this device's `Documents/scans/*/geometry.json` (matched by item `scanObjectId`s) and moves stale tunnel `/files/` URLs onto the current upload base.
  - MooveKit `Room/ColorSampler.swift`: wall, floor and per-object colors from the scan's camera keyframes (`ScanColors`). The frames are kept by `Moove/Features/Scan/ScanFrameRecorder.swift`.
  - MooveKit `NewHome/` designer layout ([decision 0300](../docs/decisions/0300-new-home-layout-design.md)): `LayoutSolver` keeps its API but delegates to `Designer` (+`Designer+Rooms.swift`): `DesignRole` classifies pieces (voxel kind + `PropPlacer.role` + name words; RoomPlan's plain "storage" box by shape; toilet/sink/bathtub by category words), beds go one per bedroom (twin/bunk pairs may share, extras in `LayoutOption.stored`, never the living room; a one-room plan takes all), then function groups are placed anchor first with a small deterministic cost search (bed headboard on a solid wall with nightstands either side and the dresser facing it; sofa facing the TV at 1.5–2.5× the diagonal with the coffee table 0.35–0.45 m in front, armchairs at its ends, lamp at the sofa's end, rug under it, TV set on its stand; dining table centered with chairs around it and 0.9 m clear; desk by a window with its chair), and `WalkGrid` (door flood fill, 10 cm cells) keeps doors connected and each piece's use side reachable. Small things are placed next to their parent so `PropPlacer` lifts them onto it. Styles differ by room use and clearances (`LayoutSolver.rules`). Look at layouts with `LayoutPreview.svg`: `MOOVE_LAYOUT_SVG_DIR=/out swift test --filter LayoutPreviewTests` (Docker: add `-v <dir>:/out -e MOOVE_LAYOUT_SVG_DIR=/out`), then `qlmanage -t -s 900 -o . *.svg`. `MovingRooms` decides which old rooms move (default newest scan per room name; New Home's and Pack's **Moving from** row (`Features/NewHome/MovingRoomsPicker.swift`: `MovingFromRow`, `MovingRoomsSheet`) opens a checklist with Select all / None, saved in UserDefaults `moove.newHome.rooms`). One source of truth: `MooveStore.movingRoomPicks` / `movingChoices` / `movingItems` (Pack: `movingItems(where:)`, which adds undecided sentimental items), so the layout, boxes, furniture notes and truck agree; bought items stay "Delivered". `MattressPairing`: a mattress whose footprint is within 15% of a bed (25% for one named a frame; not bunks) is laid on it (same spot, `onTopOf` the bed, raised) instead of counting as a second bed; `FurnitureLayer` skips drawing it (`drawnWithBed`), since the bed model has a mattress. New Home solves off the main actor (`LayoutSolver.arrange` → `arrangeAll`: every style + PropPlacer) with "Arranging your home…" over the stage, cancelled when the plan or items change, and caches results per `SolveKey` (plan, items) in `LayoutCache` so re-opening is instant.
  - MooveKit `NewHome/`: `FloorPlan`, `LayoutSolver` (three styles), `DemoHouse`, `ManualFloorPlan`, `ListingImport.swift` (`ListingLink.parse`, no network; `EstimatedFloorPlan` from beds/baths/sqft). Packing and the truck estimate are in `Packing/`.
  - App `Features/NewHome/`: `VoxelEntity.swift` (mesh -> RealityKit entity, cached per spec), `FurnitureLayer.swift` (placed items + fly-in), `NewHomeScene.swift` (the house shell + room labels), `FloorPlan3DView.swift` (takes sampled `wallColor`/`floorColor`), `VoxelThumbnail.swift`, `ListingImportView.swift` + `ListingImportClient.swift` (worker `POST /homes/import`). The scan's dollhouse is `Moove/Shared/ScanResultsView.swift`, fed by `Features/Scan/ScanFinishedResults.swift` and `Features/Room/RoomGeometry+Dollhouse.swift`.
- **Dollhouse walls:** interior walls are cut at 1.1 m (`NewHomeScene.cutawayHeightM`). Each exterior facing has two looks, split off the base by `VoxelHouse.buildLayers` into `Layer` pieces (`tall` or low, with the `facings` they belong to): tall is one continuous wall up to `VoxelHouse.displayWallHeightM` (the plan's `wallHeightM`, a scan's measured height, 2.6 m for the demo house; a scan over 3.0 m is drawn at 2.8 m) with windows glazed from the sill to a single frame at 2.05 m (`openingTopM`), doors open to 2.05 m and curtains/rods hung at the window head; low is today's cut-away look (cap row at 1.1 m, window frame bar under it, curtains from a rod at the cut). The base holds only voxels both looks share, so neither look shows the other's cap/frame rows, and `base` + low pieces == `VoxelHouse.build`. Corner columns shared by two facings are their own piece (both facings), never duplicated. `FloorPlan3DView`'s coordinator calls `NewHomeScene.setViewDirection` on every camera update (drag, auto-orbit, reset, reframe), which only toggles `isEnabled` by `Layer.isShown`: a tall piece is up while any of its facings points away from the camera (`Facing.isBack`, dot < -0.2), its low twin otherwise, so the back walls stand full height and the near ones stay low. `VoxelHouseLayersTests` checks no voxel is drawn twice at any yaw. Interior partitions always stay at the cut (a tall one would hide the room behind it). Wall art (`PropPlacer.wallTopM`) stays under the cut on every wall, since any exterior wall can become a near wall.
- **Room shots** (New Home, under Layout; `Features/NewHome/RoomShots.swift`): one card per furnished room. `FloorPlan3DView(isolatedRoom:)` shows only that room: MooveKit `FloorPlan.isolating(_:)` makes it a one-room plan with its doors/windows (so every wall gets tall/low looks), `RoomShot.placements` keeps its pieces, and `RoomShot.viewDirection`/`yaw` puts the camera at the corner opposite the two most solid walls (length minus door gaps, outside walls +15%, 30-60° between them), so those two stand tall and the near two are cut low. Shot camera: pitch 27°, FOV 32°, target 0.9 m up, warmer sun over the camera's left shoulder, no labels, no fly-in. `onSnapshot` / `snapshotToken`: the coordinator snapshots (`ARView.snapshot`) once the shell is built (`NewHomeScene.onBuilt`) and the pieces are in, and retakes it if the plan or placements change mid-capture. The gallery renders one room at a time (a 2× sized ARView scaled into the card), 3 s after the main fly-in starts, caching per `RoomShotKey` (room, its plan, placements and items), so a style switch only re-renders rooms that changed. Tap a card: `RoomShotViewer` (paged by room, **Orbit** for the live view, **Use this angle** re-snapshots, **Share** a PNG via `RoomPhoto`). Identifiers `newhome.roomShot.<room>`, `newhome.roomShot.share`; under XCUITest the snapshots may be blank.
- **Render budget (#224):** MooveKit `RenderBudgetTests` meshes the demo items, every voxel kind at its default size, the demo bedroom dollhouse and the demo house with its laid-out items. Today (triangles; the shell counts the base plus the wall pieces shown at the worst camera yaw): biggest item the bed ~10.7k, bedroom 27.6k (shell 3.5k + 12 items), house 38.4k (shell 15.7k + 8 items), all built in ~0.4-1.1 s on Linux debug. Budgets: 20k per item, 60k bedroom, 50k house, 5 s build. If a change trips one, shrink the geometry (fewer color speckles, merged faces) before raising the number; `report()` prints the current numbers.
- **Pack boxes:** MooveKit `PackingPlanner.consolidate` fills boxes after the first per-room/kind/fragility grouping (compatible kinds share, soft things pad fragile ones, small → medium → large before adding a box, a box under 40% full joins another; never books with fragile, kitchen/bathroom only with their own kind, the tote and keep-with-you box alone). Typical everyday lines are `BoxContent.estimated` (`MovingBox.estimatedOnly`); typical books count once per scanned room. The demo is 9 boxes. PackView shows one row per room (`pack.room.<room>`, `pack.room.openFirst`) that opens to its cards (`pack.box.<id>`), scanned boxes first, estimate-only ones under "Everyday stuff (estimate)" (`pack.estimates.<room>`).
- **Truck in 3D** (on Pack's Truck and crew card, non-interactive `TruckSceneView(interactive: false)`; **Full screen** opens it, `Features/Pack/TruckView.swift`): MooveKit `Packing/TruckLoader.swift` loads the plan's on-truck furniture (`plan.furniture` minus "Keep with you" car pieces) and boxes (minus keep-with-you) into `TruckSize.cargoM` (inner L×W×H per size): greedy extreme-point packing, furniture biggest first against the cab, mattresses and bed frames on edge, other furniture tips only when no upright spot fits (appliances never), boxes heaviest first then fragile then the open-first tote by the door; only boxes stack, on boxes or flat-topped case goods, never on a lighter fragile box. Without an explicit truck it moves up a size when shapes don't fit the volume estimate (the demo plan is a cargo van by volume, a 10 ft truck in 3D; `TruckLoad.sizeNote(planned:)` then shows "Fits a cargo van by volume; packs best in a 10 ft truck." on both the Truck and crew card and the 3D legend, and PackView hands its load to TruckView so both show the same truck). `Voxel/VoxelTruck.swift` builds the cut-away voxel truck (`VoxelTruck.frame/center(of:in:)` map cargo coords to the grid; cargo x = 0 is the cab wall) and `VoxelMovingBox` the boxes, taped in `PackingPalette.color(forRoom:)` (the same room colors as the 2D box cards and labels). `TruckRenderBudgetTests`: shells ≤ 4k triangles (26 ft ~1.1k), demo load ~19.4k ≤ 60k.

